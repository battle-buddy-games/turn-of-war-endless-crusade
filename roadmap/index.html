Roadmap

What we’re working on for Turn of War: Endless Crusade’s Steam Early Access launch, in the order we’re tackling it. No dates below are commitments – this page reflects real, current status, updated as work lands. Status categories: In Progress (actively being worked), Planning (scoped but not yet started, or still being analyzed), Complete (shipped, only minor follow-up remains), Needs Scoping (net-new work with no design/implementation yet), Needs Decision (a real open question with no chosen path yet), and Also Tracked (real Early Access blockers that aren’t part of the headline list below, but matter just as much).

Path to Early Access

The sequence of work driving Turn of War: Endless Crusade toward Steam Early Access, in the order we're tackling it.

1
In Progress

Ragdoll Brawling Details

Nearly done. We fixed the momentum transfer, prewarm, and interpolation issues that caused a visible jerk when ragdolls kick in during a fight, plus a batch of profile-asset bugs. Remaining work: impact force on death, arrow sticking, and a clean single-blow brawl demo (issue #146), and the last of the core fixes (issue #110). The earlier trigger-cause/respawn/UI-overlap pass is already shipped (issue #145, closed).

2
In Progress

Wider Brawls (Step-Back, Step-Forward, Group Swing)

Polishing how larger group fights read and feel – soldiers stepping back and forward as a brawl plays out, and group-swing timing that doesn’t dissolve formations too quickly (issue #125). The plumbing work can move forward now; the part that changes how troops blend back into formation is waiting on a design sign-off before it ships.

3
In Progress

UI Polish

HUD, troop cards, demo flow, and the rewards screen – a broad pass to make the interface feel finished rather than functional. This is ongoing checklist work rather than a single feature, and we’re chipping away at it alongside everything else.

4
Needs Scoping

The Start Quest

This is genuinely early. Turning the opening quest into a real, guided first experience needs a campaign progression system that doesn’t exist yet, and the campaign game mode itself is still an empty stub. We’re treating this honestly as design-and-scoping work, not a polish pass (issue #88).

5
Planning

Terrain, Environment Effects & Visibility

Sky, day/night, and seasonal visibility work to make the battlefield read better and feel more alive (issue #136). That issue is currently a planning container – it’s expected to be broken down into per-phase sub-issues before real implementation starts, so this is still in the analysis stage, not yet underway.

6
Complete

Group Movement & Formations

Powerful group selection, group movement, and formation control shipped (issue #111, closed, reviewed and approved). Only minor, non-blocking follow-up items remain – this one’s essentially done.

7
In Progress

Multiplayer

Double-checking multiplayer end to end. Done when two players can complete a full session – from lobby to results – without connectivity issues, desync, or gameplay-blocking bugs, community members can run their own server builds, and the whole experience feels intentional rather than provisional.

8
In Progress

AI Upgrades (Spawn, Engage, Ranged vs. Melee)

Improving how enemies spawn, engage, and choose between ranged and melee behavior, so encounters feel smarter and fights read more clearly.

9
In Progress

Building & Economic Bits

A dynamic production economy layered onto tactical combat – build, defend, and expand your foothold between waves. Treasury and wave-progress refactors are currently in flight.

10
Needs Decision

First Batch of AI-Generated Character Art

We haven’t decided how we’re producing character art yet. The real options on the table are coder-driven modelling, cleaned-up AI-generated base meshes, or bringing on a contributor for hero assets under deferred-compensation terms. Iterating on this during Early Access is a reasonable strategy, but no path has actually been chosen – this is an open decision, not work in progress.

11
Planning

A Short Reveal Trailer (60-90s)

A short trailer to introduce the game, built on a git-reproducible terrain capture setup so we can regenerate footage as the game changes (issue #90). Scope is confirmed; this is currently a solo effort, which is a real risk to timeline.

Also Tracked

Steam Store Page, Demo & Pricing

Not on the headline list above, but a real launch blocker: the Steam store page, a playable demo, and pricing all need to be ready before Early Access can go live.

Also Tracked

Analytics

Telemetry and analytics coverage so we can actually see how players are experiencing the game once it’s live – a real Early Access blocker, tracked separately from the headline gameplay work above.

Also Tracked

Design & Product Docs

The design and product documentation that needs to exist before launch – store copy, support material, and the internal reference docs that keep the team aligned.

Also Tracked

Playtest Build Milestone

A dedicated playtest build milestone – getting a stable, representative build in front of players ahead of Early Access, separate from day-to-day development builds.
