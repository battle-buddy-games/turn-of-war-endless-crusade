Historical Crusades

The nine Historical Crusade chapters set between the Guide Stages, and which are designed yet.

Between the Guide Stages sit the Historical Crusade chapters, where continent-scale history tests what the guide has taught. 9 chapters are planned; 1 is designed so far, and the rest still need design. No Crusade step completes through real play yet.

Historical Crusade I – The First Crusade

PlacementAfter Guide 5 (Growing Influence), before Guide 6 (Controlling the Field)
Design section§19
StatusDesigned (Leaning in the design)

The impossible expedition. This is the first moment when the player's regional game collides with continent-scale history. The Crusade should be a physical world phenomenon. Armies are raised by appropriate powers, move through actual routes, consume supplies, suffer losses and eventually reach the eastern theatre. Do not spawn a Crusader army beside the player's objective because the quest requires one.

Exam lesson: civilisation infrastructure can project power, but conquest immediately creates a holding problem.
