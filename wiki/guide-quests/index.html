Guide Quests

The guide chain that teaches a new commander, chapter by chapter, and how each step completes.

The guide chain walks a new commander from the landing at the ruins to a capital: 45 steps in 5 chapters, played in order. This page is generated from the game's own quest catalogue.

Status

StatusStepsMeaning
Live41Real play completes the step.
Any building4The step names a building the game does not have yet, so finishing any building completes it.
Stub0The step’s trigger is recorded but nothing in the game reports it yet.
